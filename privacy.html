Privacy Policy — Simple Process LLC Gate Access Service

This private residential gate-access service collects authorized residents’ mobile phone numbers only to verify access requests and send operational gate-access messages.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency varies and is limited to operational gate-access confirmations or error notifications. Message and data rates may apply.

Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP or by asking the administrator to remove their number from the access list. For help, reply HELP or contact the administrator.
