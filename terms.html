Terms and Conditions — Simple Process LLC Gate Access Service

This is a private residential gate-access service for authorized household residents.

By providing a mobile phone number and using the service, the resident agrees to receive operational SMS messages related to gate access. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out, reply STOP or ask the administrator to remove the number from the access list. For help, reply HELP or contact the administrator.

The service is provided for convenience and may be unavailable due to mobile-network, internet, cloud-service, or device failures. Access is limited to authorized residents and may be revoked at any time.
